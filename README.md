## Business problem
Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded two weeks of daily sales and a rough stock list in a notebook. She wants to know how the business is doing, whether it is growing, which products to restock, and where to focus her effort. This project uses core Python only to analyse her sales and stock data and turn it into a performance report and recommendations.

## How to run
[Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/SWNgugi/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

## Top three findings
1. Sales grew **10.7%** week-on-week (KES 22,050 → KES 24,400).
2. Weekends average **KES 4,900** versus **KES 2,685** on weekdays, which is **82.5%** higher.
3. **Tomatoes (18 kg vs 25 kg)** and **Sukuma (9 kg vs 15 kg)** are below their reorder levels. Sukuma also has a 40.0% gross margin.
